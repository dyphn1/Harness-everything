### REQ-GRPH-010: Draw vertices

Acceptance: every declared vertex is drawn.

### REQ-GRPH-011: Draw edges

Acceptance: every declared edge is drawn.

### REQ-GRPH-012: Preserve direction

Acceptance: directed edges retain their direction.

### REQ-GRPH-013: Support labels

Acceptance: labels remain readable.

### REQ-GRPH-014: Support grouping

Acceptance: groups remain visually distinct.

### REQ-GRPH-015: Support selection

Acceptance: selected items are exposed to callers.

### REQ-GRPH-016: Support keyboard access

Acceptance: every action is keyboard reachable.

### REQ-GRPH-017: Preserve layout

Acceptance: saved layouts can be restored.

### REQ-GRPH-018: Export the graph

Acceptance: exports include the complete graph.

### REQ-GRPH-020 (replaces: REQ-GRPH-019; aliases: REQ-GRAPH-020): Report export errors

Acceptance: failures include a recovery action and error identifier.
